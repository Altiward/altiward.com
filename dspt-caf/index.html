CAF-aligned DSPT

Evidence for admin rights on your Macs.

For organisations assessed against the CAF-aligned Data Security and Protection Toolkit (DSPT), B2.c covers privileged access management and C1 covers security monitoring. Altiward contributes Mac admin session records and findings to support your assessment.

Last reviewed October 2026

Where Altiward helps

The outcomes it gives you evidence for.

Based on NHS England's CAF-aligned DSPT guidance for B2 identity and access control and C1 security monitoring.

OutcomeWhat the guidance saysWhat Altiward gives you
B2.c Privileged user managementManage privileged access closely, know who holds it, and revoke it when it is no longer needed.Agent checks and configurable enforcement against recorded grant expiry, plus session records showing the user, Mac, recorded reason, and when and how the session ended
B2.c, logging privileged usePrivileged user actions should be logged so they can be independently reviewed and audited.Session records and detected changes to admin accounts and rights, for independent review. Correlate these with activity logs; Altiward does not provide a complete record of privileged actions
C1.a Sources and tools for logging and monitoringInclude relevant host logs and user activity in your monitoring coverage.Each Mac's admin sessions and findings, sent as OCSF events to Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel or a TLS or TCP syslog receiver
C1.c Generating alertsIdentify potential incidents in monitoring data and trigger alerts where appropriate.Findings for unexpected admin rights, detected admin-account creation, overdue rights and missing check-ins, with companion SIEM rules
C1.f Understanding user and system behaviour, and threat intelligenceUse knowledge of normal behaviour and relevant threat intelligence to help detect incidents.Session durations and unexpected-admin findings to review alongside your existing monitoring and threat intelligence
Who it's for

Health and care teams with Macs.

01

Assessed against the CAF-aligned DSPT

NHS organisations, and IT suppliers to health and care, completing the DSPT in its CAF-aligned form.

02

Managing Macs with an MDM

Mac fleets managed through an MDM such as Jamf or Intune, where people sometimes need admin and SAP Privileges is how they get it.

03

Keeping the record in-house

You run the Altiward server on your own infrastructure. Events go from your Macs to your server and on to your SIEM, not to us.

Questions

What security and IG leads ask.

Does Altiward make us DSPT compliant?

No tool can. Altiward gives you evidence for B2.c and C1 on your Mac devices.

Which version of the guidance is this based on?

NHS England's CAF-aligned DSPT guidance, as checked in October 2026. Check the current wording on the DSPT site before you rely on it.

Does the record leave our network?

You choose. The server, database and console run on your infrastructure. If you configure an external SIEM, it receives the exported events. Altiward's session evidence is sent to your server and chosen SIEM, not to us.

Is Altiward linked to NHS England?

No. Altiward isn't affiliated with or endorsed by NHS England or the NCSC.
