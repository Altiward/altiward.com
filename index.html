Oversight for temporary macOS admin rights

Altiward checks each Mac's admin group against the grants SAP Privileges records, flags admins nobody granted, and can remove them, along with rights that outlive their grant. It's open source and runs on your own infrastructure.

Why SAP Privileges' timer isn't enough

SAP Privileges gives admin rights for a set time, and a countdown in the person's own session takes them back. Once someone is admin, they can stop that countdown, and nothing on the Mac removes the rights. By default, Altiward's agent alerts when the timer is stopped, and, if enabled, removes rights that outlive their grant.

Which project do you need?

Altiward and the forwarder send OCSF records. Add the SIEM detections that match your event source.

  • Who holds admin on each Mac now, and what happens when rights outlive their grantaltiward
  • SAP Privileges' grants and revocations in your SIEM, without an agentaltiward-forwarder
  • Alerts in Sentinel or Splunk on the events either project sendsaltiward-siem

Need to know when rights outlive their timer? The forwarder passes on what Privileges reports, so it can't see a stopped timer. That takes the Altiward agent.
